# Known Risks

## Scope and evidence conventions

This is a static, source-code audit. No application workflow, queue worker, scheduler, browser session, export, or test suite was executed. `SOURCE_VERIFIED` means that the cited condition is directly present in the repository; it does not mean the resulting behavior has been reproduced at runtime.

Risk labels used here are `SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `INFERRED_FROM_CODE`, `NEEDS_RUNTIME_VERIFICATION`, `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`, `SCAFFOLDED_ONLY`, `UNUSED`, and `UNKNOWN`.

## Plausible concurrency risks

These are not reported as reproduced defects. They are transaction/interleaving risks inferred from the absence or placement of guards and locks.

| ID | Scenario | Source basis | Possible outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CR-01 | Two restocks or adjustments update the same raw material concurrently. | `RawMaterialController::storeRestock` and `storeAdjustment` use transactions but do not reload the material with `lockForUpdate()` before calculating new stock. | Lost stock updates or ledger snapshots that do not match final stock. | `INFERRED_FROM_CODE`, `NEEDS_RUNTIME_VERIFICATION` |
| CR-02 | Stock changes between the production sufficiency check and completion deduction. | `ProductionController::complete` checks availability before its transaction and does not lock raw-material rows during the check/deduction sequence. | Over-consumption, negative/incorrect stock, or completion based on stale availability. | `INFERRED_FROM_CODE`, `NEEDS_RUNTIME_VERIFICATION` |

## Scope gaps that are not defects by themselves

- ROP is `SCAFFOLDED_ONLY`: minimum-stock fields, static threshold ratios, and dashboard low-stock indicators exist, but no replenishment recommendation, lead-time, safety-stock, or order workflow is implemented.
- MRP is `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`: current BOM demand and planned/processing reservations are calculated for display, but there is no persisted material plan, procurement proposal, lead-time model, lot sizing, or release workflow.
- Reports are `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`: raw-material transactions and production summaries exist, while sales/revenue, return/disposal, finished-goods movement, and ROP/MRP reports are absent.
- Sales/returns are `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`: quantity dispatch, sold counts, returns, inspection, and disposal state changes exist, but financial sales records and immutable stock-movement history do not.

These gaps require product-owner confirmation before being treated as bugs or changed requirements.
