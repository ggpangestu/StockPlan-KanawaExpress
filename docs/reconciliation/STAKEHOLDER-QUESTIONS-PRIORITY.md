# Stakeholder Questions by Priority

## How to use this file

These questions are for decisions that repository source and conversation history cannot settle by themselves. The `Answer`, `Answered by`, and `Date` cells are intentionally blank. Do not fill them from inference, and do not treat a recommended default as an approved answer.

Historical statements are cited as context, not runtime or acceptance evidence. Current-implementation statements come from the static audit and have not been runtime-tested.

## Answer recording rule

When a stakeholder responds, preserve the exact question ID and add only:

- the decision or “not decided”;
- the named decision owner;
- the decision date/effective version;
- supporting business or collaborator evidence;
- exceptions; and
- related verification-register IDs.

Do not relabel the answer as repository implementation until a later source recheck finds matching code.
